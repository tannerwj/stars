    The Survey

    For millennia, astronomers joined pairs of stars and called the result a line. The Stellar Triangulation Survey asks the next question: when any three stars are joined, what figure is formed?

    Method. Select any three stars in the dome above. The instrument draws the figure, measures its sides in degrees of arc, sums its interior angles, and classifies it with full rigor.

    Findings. Every figure examined to date has been a triangle. The Survey continues, nightly.
